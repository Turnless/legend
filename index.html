01 / OUR MISSION

Make internet culture
feel like it belongs to everyone.

Our mission is to give the internet a character people can recognise, reinterpret, and make their own.

LEGEND brings together the absurdity of memes, the confidence of a legendary persona, the language of trading culture, and the belief that communities create meaning together.

We are here to make things worth sharing: original visuals, sharp humour, community-made variations, and a brand that leaves room for people to participate.

02 / THE IDEA

A little absurd.
Impossible to ignore.

A crown is a signature. The character is an invitation. The story is still being written.

Every corner of the internet has its characters. LEGEND is an original crowned meme figure designed for remixing, sharing, and creative community participation.

Part confidence, part internet humour, part market-culture self-awareness. The character can be a trader one day, a philosopher the next, and the punchline in somebody else's meme tomorrow.
