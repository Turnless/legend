Where can I buy $LGD?

On Pons, the launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Always check the contract address: 0x165dc537ec67ce2c033bcfcac562e6c311c7c213. You need ETH on Robinhood Chain to pay and for gas.

Can the community create its own memes?

Yes, that is the point. Download the meme pack and remix it. The meme contest rewards the best remakes of the 20 official memes.
